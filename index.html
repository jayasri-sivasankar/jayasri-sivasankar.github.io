I am a statistician with a background in biostatistics, currently working in real-world evidence and healthcare analytics. My work involves using statistical methods to understand and generate insights from real-world healthcare data, particularly in areas such as survival analysis, causal inference, and oncology.

I am particularly interested in developing rigorous approaches for generating reliable evidence from real-world healthcare data and addressing methodological challenges in observational research.
