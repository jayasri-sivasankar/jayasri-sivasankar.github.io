About

I am a statistician and healthcare data scientist currently working as a Data Scientist — RWE at Molecular Connections Analytics in Bengaluru. My work is focused on real-world evidence and healthcare analytics, with an emphasis on applying statistical and computational approaches to observational healthcare data.

I completed my Five-Year Integrated M.Sc. in Statistics at Pondicherry University in 2023, followed by a PG Diploma in Biostatistics at ACTREC, Tata Memorial Centre. During my academic and early professional training, I developed an interest in biostatistics and clinical research, particularly in the application of statistical methods to oncology and healthcare data.

In 2023, I interned in the Biostatistics R&D Division at GlaxoSmithKline Pharmaceuticals in Bengaluru, where I gained exposure to the application of statistics in pharmaceutical research. I subsequently worked as a Biostatistician Trainee at ACTREC, where my experience in clinical research and biostatistics further strengthened my interest in healthcare research.

My current interests lie at the intersection of real-world evidence, causal inference, survival and longitudinal analysis, and healthcare data standardization. I am particularly interested in methodological challenges involved in generating reliable evidence from observational healthcare data and in developing reproducible approaches for healthcare analytics.

Alongside my professional work, I enjoy developing independent statistical and data-oriented projects. These include R Shiny applications for research workflows, tools for extracting structured information from documents, and an ongoing project exploring vocabulary mapping and data standardization within the OMOP Common Data Model.

I am currently exploring PhD opportunities that would allow me to further develop my interests in real-world evidence and healthcare analytics and contribute to methodological research in this area.

Outside of work and research

Outside of work and research, I enjoy learning French, journaling, photography, reading books, watching sunsets and sunrises, and exploring new places. I also enjoy exploring new ideas in data science and statistics and continuously learning through academic workshops and independent projects.
