About

I am a statistician and healthcare data scientist with an academic background in statistics and biostatistics. I currently work in real-world evidence and healthcare analytics, where I use statistical and computational methods to work with observational healthcare data.

My interest in healthcare research developed through my experiences in biostatistics, clinical research, and real-world evidence. I completed my Five-Year Integrated M.Sc. in Statistics at Pondicherry University in 2023, followed by a Postgraduate Diploma in Biostatistics at ACTREC, Tata Memorial Centre.

During my internship in the Biostatistics Research and Development Division at GlaxoSmithKline in Bengaluru, I gained exposure to the application of statistics in pharmaceutical research. I subsequently worked as a Biostatistician Trainee at ACTREC, where my experience in clinical and oncology research further strengthened my interest in applying statistical methods to healthcare questions.

My current interests lie at the intersection of real-world evidence, causal inference, survival and longitudinal analysis, oncology research, and healthcare data standardisation . I am particularly interested in the methodological challenges involved in generating reliable evidence from observational healthcare data and in developing reproducible approaches for healthcare analytics.

Alongside my professional work, I enjoy developing independent statistical and data-oriented projects. These include R Shiny applications for research workflows, tools for extracting structured information from documents, and an ongoing project exploring vocabulary mapping and data standardisation within the OMOP Common Data Model.

I am currently exploring PhD opportunities that would allow me to further develop my interests in real-world evidence and healthcare analytics and contribute to methodological research in this area.

Outside of work and research

Outside of work and research, I enjoy learning French, journaling, photography, reading books, watching sunsets and sunrises, and exploring new places. I also enjoy exploring new ideas in data science and statistics and continuously learning through academic workshops and independent projects.
