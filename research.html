Research

My research interests lie at the intersection of statistics, real-world evidence, and healthcare analytics. I am particularly interested in methodological challenges in using observational healthcare data to generate reliable clinical evidence, including causal inference, time-to-event analysis, longitudinal data, treatment effects, and healthcare data standardization.

Master's Thesis

Applying Causal Inference and Machine Learning in Survival Analysis in Healthcare

M.Sc. Statistics Thesis, Pondicherry University · Project conducted at GlaxoSmithKline (GSK), Bengaluru · 2023

Explored causal inference and machine learning approaches for survival analysis in healthcare, with a focus on Targeted Maximum Likelihood Estimation (TMLE) and One-Step TMLE for time-to-event outcomes. Conducted simulation studies comparing traditional causal inference approaches with TMLE-based methods.
