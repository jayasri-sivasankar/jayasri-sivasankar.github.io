Research Experience

Applying Causal Inference and Machine Learning in Survival Analysis in Healthcare

M.Sc. Statistics Project · Pondicherry University · May 2023
Project conducted at GlaxoSmithKline (GSK), Bengaluru · January–July 2023

View thesis

My Master's research explored the application of causal inference and machine learning methods to survival analysis in healthcare. The project focused on methodological approaches for estimating treatment effects from observational data and understanding how causal methods can be integrated with time-to-event analysis.

The work examined traditional causal inference approaches, including g-computation and propensity score methods, before exploring Targeted Maximum Likelihood Estimation (TMLE) and its application to time-to-event data. The project also examined One-Step TMLE, which targets the survival curve as a whole and addresses the non-monotonicity issue associated with pointwise estimation of survival probabilities.

A simulation study was conducted to compare the performance of multivariable regression, propensity score-based inverse probability weighting, g-computation, and TMLE. The study used simulated healthcare variables representing baseline and time-varying covariates, treatment, outcomes, and censoring, with repeated simulations used to evaluate the different approaches.

This work strengthened my interest in causal inference, survival analysis, targeted learning, and the application of statistical and machine learning methods to observational healthcare data.

Current Research Direction

My current interests are increasingly focused on the methodological and applied challenges of real-world evidence. I am particularly interested in how statistical and computational approaches can be used to generate reliable evidence from observational healthcare data while addressing issues such as confounding, time-varying information, longitudinal measurements, and complex healthcare data structures.

I am interested in pursuing doctoral research that combines rigorous statistical methodology with meaningful healthcare applications, particularly in areas where real-world data can contribute to better research and evidence generation.
