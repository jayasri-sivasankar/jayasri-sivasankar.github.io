Research Interests

My research interests are centred around the use of statistical and computational methods to address questions in healthcare research. Research Experience

Applying Causal Inference and Machine Learning in Survival Analysis in Healthcare

M.Sc. Statistics Thesis · Pondicherry University · 2023

My Master's research explored the application of causal inference and machine learning approaches to survival analysis in healthcare. The work provided an opportunity to study methods for estimating treatment effects in time-to-event settings and to evaluate statistical approaches through simulation-based analysis.

This experience strengthened my interest in survival analysis, causal inference, and the application of statistical methodology to healthcare research.

Current Research Direction

My current interests are increasingly focused on the methodological and applied challenges of real-world evidence. I am particularly interested in how statistical and computational approaches can be used to generate reliable evidence from observational healthcare data while addressing issues such as confounding, time-varying information, longitudinal measurements, and complex healthcare data structures.

I am interested in pursuing doctoral research that combines rigorous statistical methodology with meaningful healthcare applications, particularly in areas where real-world data can contribute to better research and evidence generation.
